# The map

This game is on Mundi Fusion: Lekmap's Pangaea, torn apart along rifts.

- Rifts run across the land and usually split it into several continents. They are narrow seas, with necks of high ground and peninsulas reaching into them, and islands in them and in the ocean around.
- Shallow fords join the continents, usually two or three to each, so armies, settlers and caravans can walk from any civilization to any other. A ford is water shallow enough to walk across without embarking, at 2 moves a tile; ships sail through it, no city can be founded on it, and a unit standing on it defends worse.
- Only the water next to land is shallow. Beyond it the sea is deep, and crossing deep water needs Astronomy. Until then, ships and embarked units keep to the coasts and cross a rift where it is narrow or at a ford; islands close to a coast stay within reach.
- Most civilizations get long coastlines, so harbours, sea trade and navies reach much of the map, while the fords keep land routes and land wars open as well.
- Mountains run in ranges with passes between them; none stand on a coast.
